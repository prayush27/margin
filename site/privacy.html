Privacy

Last updated November 15, 2026

Margin is built so your study notes never leave your computer. This page explains exactly what it stores and the one situation in which it goes online.

What Margin stores

Your courses, terms, definitions, quiz progress, recent PDFs and settings are saved in Chrome's local extension storage, inside your own browser profile. There is no Margin account, no server and no sync. Uninstalling Margin deletes everything, so use Settings → Export if you want a backup.

What Margin sends over the internet

Only one feature makes network requests: Find one, on the card you see when saving a term. While it is open, Margin asks these public services for definitions of that term:

ServiceWhat is sent
WikipediaThe term and a subject word, for example “heap computer science”.
WiktionaryThe term.
EMBL-EBI Ontology Lookup ServiceThe term, for statistics, biology, chemistry and medicine courses.
MeSH, U.S. National Library of MedicineThe term, for biology, medicine and psychology courses.

Nothing else is sent: not the page you are on, its text, your course names, your glossary or any identifier. Answers are cached on your computer. Textbook (OpenStax) and computer science (NIST) definitions are built into the extension and need no connection. If you only use Write my own, Margin makes no network requests at all.

When you open a web PDF, Margin's viewer downloads it from its original address, the same way Chrome's built-in viewer would.

Send feedback opens this website’s feedback page with Margin’s version number in the link (for example ?v=0.1.0), so bug reports say which version you have. Nothing else is included.

Suggested terms in the PDF viewer are found on your computer, by comparing the PDF's text with glossaries and word lists built into Margin. The PDF's text is never sent anywhere, and there's no AI service involved.

Permissions

PermissionWhy Margin needs it
StorageTo keep your glossary on your computer, without a size cap for big courses.
Access to websitesTo underline your saved terms on school and learning sites (and any site you turn on), to save a term from any page, and to fetch definitions for Find one. On other sites Margin stays inactive by default.
Context menusFor the right-click items: “Save to Margin” for selected text, “Open PDF in Margin” for PDF links, and the toolbar icon’s menu.
Side panelFor the Class sidebar, which lists your saved terms for one class beside the page you’re reading. It reads the page’s text on your computer to see which terms are on it; nothing is sent anywhere.
Web requestsTo notice when a tab opens a PDF, so it can open in Margin's viewer. Only used after you turn on Open PDFs in Margin.

No tracking

Margin has no analytics, no ads, no remote code and no third-party scripts. This website doesn't use cookies or analytics either.

Questions

Margin is open source, so you can read every line on GitHub. For questions, send feedback.
