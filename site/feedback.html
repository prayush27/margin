Send feedback

Margin is in beta, and you're one of the first people using it. Tell me what broke, what confused you, or what you wish it did. Short is fine.

Quick form Two minutes, no account needed. Report a bug Something didn't work. Opens a GitHub issue (needs a GitHub account). Suggest an idea Something Margin should do. Also on GitHub.

What helps most

  • What you were doing (for example, “reading a lecture PDF from Canvas”).
  • What you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if something looked wrong.

GitHub issues are public, so please don't include private links, your name or anything from a course you're not allowed to share.
